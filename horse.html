🐴 My Favorite Horses 🐴

Spending time with my horses is one of my favorite ways to feel close to God. Every horse shows His creativity, strength, and beauty. Job 39:19–20 reminds me that God is the one who gives the horse its strength and beauty, and I love seeing His handiwork in every detail. 🐴✨

Riding, grooming, and caring for them reminds me that God cares about all His creation, big and small. Even simple moments—like brushing a mane or feeding hay—can be done with love and purpose. Colossians 3:23 teaches me to do everything with my whole heart, as if I’m doing it for the Lord.

Horses bring me peace, joy, and a sense of wonder. They remind me to slow down, be gentle, and appreciate the beauty God placed in the world around me.

Thanks for visiting! May God bless your day with joy, love, and His peace 🌾🐴✨
