My Favorite Horses

Spending time with my horses is one of my favorite ways to feel close to God. Every horse shows His creativity, strength, and beauty. Job 39:19-20 says, “Do you give the horse its strength? Do you clothe its neck with a flowing mane?” I love seeing God’s handiwork in their strong legs, shiny coats, and gentle eyes. 🐴✨

Riding, grooming, and caring for them reminds me that God cares about all His creation, big and small. Even little things like brushing a mane, feeding hay, or giving a gentle pat feel like ways to honor Him. Colossians 3:23 says, “Whatever you do, work at it with all your heart, as working for the Lord, not for human masters.” Every hoofbeat and happy whinny is a reminder of God’s joy and creativity, and spending time with them fills my heart with peace, gratitude, and wonder.

Thanks for visiting! May God bless your day with joy, love, and His peace 🌾🐴✨
