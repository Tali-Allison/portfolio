Faith in Jesus ✝️

My faith means everything to me. I believe in Jesus Christ, and I try my best to follow Him every day. The Bible is like my guide for life, and it helps me when I feel happy, confused, or even worried.

One of my favorite verses is Hebrews 13:5-6: “For He Himself has said, ‘I will never leave you nor forsake you.’ So we may boldly say: ‘The Lord is my helper; I will not fear. What can man do to me?’” I really like this because it reminds me that Jesus is always with me, even when I feel alone.

Another verse I love is 2 Corinthians 5:7: “For we walk by faith, not by sight.” That helps me remember that even when I don’t understand what’s happening, I can still trust God’s plan.

“The Lord is my shepherd; I shall not want.” – Psalm 23:1

“I can do all things through Christ who strengthens me.” – Philippians 4:13

“Let your light so shine before men, that they may see your good works and glorify your Father in heaven.” – Matthew 5:16

“Casting all your care upon Him, for He cares for you.” – 1 Peter 5:7

I feel like Jesus gives me so much peace and hope. He teaches me to be kind, forgive others, and trust Him even when things are hard. I know I’m not perfect, but I really try to follow Him and grow in my faith.

Romans 12:12 says: “Rejoicing in hope, patient in tribulation, continuing steadfastly in prayer.” That verse helps me remember to keep going and keep trusting God no matter what happens.

Thanks for visiting! May God bless your day with joy, love, and His peace 🌾🐴✨
