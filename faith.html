✝️ Faith in Jesus ✝️

My faith in Jesus means everything to me. It’s not just something I believe on Sundays—it’s something I carry with me every single day. When I feel happy, I thank Him. When I feel confused, I ask Him for wisdom. When I feel nervous or overwhelmed, I remember that God is still in control.

The Bible is like a guidebook for my life. It brings me peace, direction, and hope when I need it most. I’m still learning, still growing, and still trying my best to trust Him more each day.

🌾 Scriptures That Encourage Me

Hebrews 13:5–6

Let your conduct be without covetousness; be content with such things as you have. For He Himself has said, “I will never leave you nor forsake you.” So we may boldly say: “The Lord is my helper; I will not fear. What can man do to me?”

God is always with me, even when I feel alone. I can be brave because He is my helper.

2 Corinthians 5:7

For we live by faith, not by sight.

I can trust God even when I don’t understand everything happening in my life.

Psalm 23:1

The Lord is my Shepherd; I shall not want.

God takes care of me and gives me what I truly need.

Philippians 4:13

I can do all things through Christ who strengthens me.

I’m never facing things alone—Jesus gives me strength.

Matthew 5:16

Let your light so shine before men, that they may see your good works and glorify your Father in heaven.

I want my life to show God’s love through my actions.

1 Peter 5:7

Casting all your cares upon Him, for He cares for you.

I can give all my worries to God because He truly cares about me.

Romans 12:12

Rejoice in hope, be patient in tribulation, be constant in prayer.

Stay hopeful, be patient, and keep praying no matter what.

My faith isn’t perfect, but it’s real—and I’m thankful every day that God walks with me through it all.
